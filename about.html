Our Story

We Stand for Accessibility and Sustainability

Globalist Market was founded in 2021 born to provide the market in Guatemala and Central America with access to a more affordable product line that at the same time provides greener solutions and more participation of plastic producers in the region to the circular economy. Our renovated products are to the market, post-production raw materials that are sourced from the USA.

Our Purpose

Mission

Our mission is to provide our customers with the opportunity to be a real part of the solution to the plastic pollution of our world by utilizing products that provide high quality, affordable green solutions.

Vision

To establish programs and partnerships that use these products consistently, improving their cost and doing our part in the green space of plastic utilization.
